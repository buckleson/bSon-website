Seed phrases and gas fees are two of the biggest reasons ordinary users and enterprises avoid blockchain. Lose twelve words and you lose your money. Run out of a network's native token and you cannot move anything at all.

## What account abstraction changes

On Hyper 0X, accounts are smart. Instead of a single private key controlling everything, the account itself enforces the rules:

- **One face scan to pay.** Users authenticate the way they already do on their phones.
- **Limits built in.** Spending limits and approvals are enforced by the account.
- **Apps can pay the fees.** Users never need to hold gas tokens.
- **A lost phone is not a lost wallet.** Recovery is part of the account design.

## Why it matters for enterprises

For a business, account abstraction means employees and customers can use blockchain-backed apps without training, while the organisation keeps control through policies such as maker-checker approvals. Combined with Hyper Wallet SDKs, it lets you embed secure wallets into internal apps and customer products, with every action monitored and recorded on Hyper 0X.
