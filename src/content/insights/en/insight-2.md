AI adoption is inevitable. Trust is not. Enterprises in banking, healthcare and insurance want AI that can access data, call internal systems and act autonomously, but they also need to prove afterwards exactly what happened.

## Why logs are not enough

Traditional logs live on the same systems the AI touches. They can be edited, deleted or simply incomplete, and they cannot prove what the AI did or why it did it.

## A receipt for every action

On Hyper 0X, every AI decision and action is recorded as a transaction. Each one leaves a signed, chained receipt showing who did what, and when. Because each receipt links to the one before it, any gap or edit is immediately visible.

## Non-intrusive by design

Hyper 0X sits on top of existing systems and requires no change to current business logic or infrastructure. The receipts become the definitive source of truth for:

- **Compliance:** proof for regulators that controls were followed.
- **Billing:** an exact record of what each AI workload consumed.
- **Disputes:** one shared record that every party can verify.

We secure how AI runs, not what AI thinks. Pavii brings the same idea to AI agents, anchoring every agent's logs on Hyper 0X.
