Account abstraction makes a blockchain account smart. Instead of one private key controlling everything, the account itself enforces rules: users sign in with a face scan or passkey, apps can pay the fees, spending limits are built in, and a lost phone is not a lost wallet. On Hyper 0X it is the default, so users never handle seed phrases or gas tokens.

## Why do seed phrases hold blockchain back?

Seed phrases and gas fees are two of the biggest reasons people and enterprises avoid blockchain. Lose twelve words and you lose your money. Run out of a network's native token and you cannot move anything at all.

## What does account abstraction change?

- **One face scan to pay.** Users authenticate the way they already do on their phones.
- **Limits built in.** Spending limits and approvals are enforced by the account.
- **Apps can pay the fees.** Users never need to hold gas tokens.
- **A lost phone is not a lost wallet.** Recovery is part of the account design.

## Why does it matter for enterprises?

Employees and customers can use blockchain-backed apps without training, while the organisation keeps control through policies such as maker-checker approvals.
