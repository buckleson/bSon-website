Hyper 0X is Buckleson's own **permissioned L1 blockchain**, engineered from scratch. Every Buckleson product, including Hyper Wallet, the Wallet SDKs and Pavii, signs, settles and logs on Hyper 0X, so all of them write to the same record.

## Why our own L1?

Building on a public chain means inheriting its fees, its roadmap and its outages. Owning the chain lets us design for what enterprises actually need:

- **Final in one pass:** leaderless aBFT ordering, so there are no reorgs and no front-running.
- **Post-quantum today:** hybrid ML-KEM-768 on every connection, plus four layers of encryption.
- **Modular core:** add an encryption layer, L2 type, bridge or rule without a fork.
- **An L2 per customer:** each with its own design, policies and bridge, all on one shared record.
- **Dynamic blocks:** blocks resize with load, fast when quiet, no backlog at peaks.
- **Compliance as code:** limits, KYC and maker-checker approvals run before a transaction executes.
- **Connects to anything:** bridges and SDKs reach public chains, bank DLTs and APIs.

## How it is layered

The core sits at the bottom and never depends on the products built on top of it.

| Layer               | What lives here                               |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Apps                | Hyper Wallet, Pavii and customer applications |
| SDK, L2 and interop | Wallet SDKs, customer L2s, bridges            |
| Core                | Policy, ledger, consensus and cryptography    |

Four parts plug into the core through their own interfaces: [consensus](../../hyper-0x/consensus/), [encryption](../../hyper-0x/encryption/), [L2s and scaling](../../hyper-0x/l2-scaling/), and [bridges, SDKs and contracts](../../hyper-0x/bridges-contracts/).
