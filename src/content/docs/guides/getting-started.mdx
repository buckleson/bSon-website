Hyper 0X is a permissioned network: validators are named institutions, and every customer runs on their own L2. Getting started is therefore a short engagement with our team rather than a public sign-up.

## 1. Tell us about your workload

[Contact us](../../contact/) with what you want to put on-chain: tokenized assets, payments, an exchange, internal wallets or AI-agent security. We will help you decide whether you need your own L2, a Wallet SDK integration, Pavii, or a combination.

## 2. Start with a pilot

We scope a pilot around one use case, agree success metrics up front, and assign a forward-deployed engineer to work inside your stack.

## 3. Design your L2

Together we define your L2's policies (limits, KYC, approvals), privacy model, block parameters and bridges to your existing systems and public chains.

## 4. Go to production

Once the pilot meets its metrics, we scale it to a production-grade rollout, with your L2 settling to the Hyper 0X L1.
